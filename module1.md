# What are Automations?
Automation is the process of using technologies to perform tasks without human intervention, making repetitive ot time-consuming activities faster and more efficient.

# Automations:
- Follow a fixed, rule based process.
- Great for repetitive, predictable tasks.
- Example: An Email sent to every new customer as soon



# Automations are Deterministic and Predefined
Example: Automatically sending a welcome email to a new subscriber after they sign up for a newsletter.

# What are Agents ?
An AI Agent is a node that uses artificial intellegence to perform smart tasks, like analyzing text or making decisions.

- An AI agent is like a super-smart assistant.
